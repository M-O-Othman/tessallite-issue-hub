## What Was Tried and Failed

**Production outage, roughly four minutes.** Stamping `alembic_version` to
`f1127bbc1194` while the deployed image predated that migration crashed every
container at the first cold start: `Can't locate revision identified by
'f1127bbc1194'`, exit 255, failed startup probe. The reasoning error was
assuming an older image would treat an unknown newer revision as a no-op;
alembic aborts instead. `min-instances 0` meant the fault stayed latent until
a cold start rather than surfacing immediately.

Recovery: reverted the stamp to `35908fccae73` (service restored on the next
request), made the migration idempotent, verified the image end-to-end against a
database in production's exact state, then deployed and let the migration
advance the version itself. Logged as item 8 in `Docs/known_issues.md`.
